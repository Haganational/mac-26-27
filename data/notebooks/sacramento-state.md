## Who they are

Connor Briggs grew up in West Sacramento, the son of a state worker and a dental hygienist, and spent twenty years as a river guide on the American River. He started calling Hornets games from the cheap seats until The Sacramento Intelligencer gave him a column, mostly to keep him quieter. He is a river-town optimist always half a step behind the play.
