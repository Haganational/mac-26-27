Standards

  • Independent. Hardwood Bricks is not affiliated with any conference, member school or sportsbook.
  • College athletes are students. We write about what players do on the floor. We don't publish birthdates, family details or social media pile-ons.
  • Betting is kept apart. Odds screens and model tools sit in a separate section for adults 21 and over, locked behind an access code. The coverage we request credentials for is the recaps, previews and Fieldhouse Notes above.
  • Corrections are made on the page with a note saying what changed.
