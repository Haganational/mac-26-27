What we cover

  • All 12 MAC teams, from the first exhibition through the MAC tournament in Cleveland.
  • Fieldhouse Notes: game dispatches from the editor. Each is filed by how it was reported: in the arena, from the broadcast, from print and wire reports, or by phone.
  • Schedule & TV, team ratings and player ratings built from every Division I box score.
  • News gathered from every school, the conference and the wider press, always linking to and crediting the original reporting.
  • Travel: miles, rest and time zones for every team.

How often

  • The site refreshes three times a day, every day of the season.
  • Fieldhouse Notes dispatches on game nights.
  • A weekly podcast and newsletter{% if not (mh.podcast_url and mh.newsletter_url) %} (launching this season){% endif %}.

Editorial standards

  • Independent. {{ site_name }} is not affiliated with the Mid-American Conference, any member school, or any sportsbook.
  • Credit. We link to and name the outlet behind any reporting we cite, and never republish another outlet's work.
  • Labeled sourcing. Every dispatch says how it was reported. An "arena" dispatch means the editor was in the building.
  • Corrections. Errors are corrected on the page with a note saying what changed. Send corrections to the contact above.
  • Sponsorship. Any sponsored placement is labeled as sponsored. Sponsors do not see or shape coverage.
  • Betting analysis is kept separate from news coverage, is for adults 21 and over, and never promises results. If you or someone you know has a gambling problem, call 1-800-GAMBLER.
