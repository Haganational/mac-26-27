How it works

Every D-I game gives three numbers: possessions, and points per 100 possessions for each side. Each team gets an offense, defense and pace rating that together best explain all its games at once, after accounting for who it played and where. Because every opponent's rating is in the same equations, beating a good team counts for more than beating a bad one, and that opponent's rating is in turn set by everyone it played.

Projection for a game: possessions = avg pace + home pace + away pace, then each side's points = possessions × (avg efficiency + its offense + the other side's defense ± home court). Spread is the difference, total is the sum.

Recency: a game's weight halves every {{ m.params.half_life|int }} days. Blowouts: margin past {{ m.params.cap|int }} pts/100 counts at {{ (m.params.cap_slope * 100)|int }}%. Prior: last season, regressed {{ (m.params.regress * 100)|int }}% to average, worth {{ m.params.prior_games|round(1) }} games. Non-D-I games: count {{ (m.params.non_d1_weight * 100)|int }}%, opponents pooled. Win %: normal with sd {{ m.params.sigma_margin }} pts around the projected margin. Possessions: FGA − OREB + TO + 0.475 × FTA from ESPN box scores.
