How it works

  • The source. ESPN's play-by-play for every D-I game: every scoring play with the running score and the clock, and every free throw, made or missed.
  • Comeback Kings. From 10:00 left in the second half on, we track the winner's deficit after every basket. The biggest one is the comeback. The "Clock" column is the time left when it was biggest. A team appears once, with its best late comeback; "Late comebacks" counts every win it trailed in over that stretch.
  • Foul Him. Only free throws after halftime count. A game is used only when its free-throw log adds up to the box score's made and attempted totals for both teams{% if n_ft %} ({{ n_ft_ok }} of {{ n_ft }} games so far){% endif %}, so a garbled feed never moves a number.
  • Early season. Twenty attempts is a small sample. Expect the list to move a lot until conference play.
