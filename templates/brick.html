How the Brick is chosen

Every game a {{ LG }} team finishes from Monday morning to the next Monday morning is a candidate, as long as the team took at least {{ rule.MIN_FGA }} shots from the floor. The Brick goes to the lowest true shooting percentage: points divided by twice the shooting attempts, where a free throw attempt counts as 0.44 of a shot. It counts twos, threes and free throws together, so there's no hiding a cold night behind the foul line. Ties go to the team with more missed shots, free throws included.

Guarantee games don't count. A night against an {{ rule.POWER[:-1]|join(', ') }} or {{ rule.POWER[-1] }} team is left out: everybody shoots poorly on a power-conference floor, and an award for that would just list who took the biggest check. The award is frozen Monday at 6 a.m. Eastern and never changes after that.

We name teams, never players, and the write-up always finds the bright spot. One cold night says nothing about anybody. It's a brick, not a verdict.
