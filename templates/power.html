How the Bricks Index works

Recent form is our ratings model, adjusted for opponent and venue, fit again with a short memory: a game's weight halves every {{ snap.method.half_life_days|int }} days, so the last three weeks carry the number. Road-win bonus: every win away from home adds {{ '%.1f'|format(snap.method.road_bonus_pts) }} points for beating an average D-I team on its floor, more for a better opponent, less for a weaker one, fading on the same {{ snap.method.half_life_days|int }}-day clock. A neutral-site win counts {{ (snap.method.neutral_share * 100)|int }}%. Both are in points per game. The ranking is frozen Monday morning; capsules are written from that week's facts.
